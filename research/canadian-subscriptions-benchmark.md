# Canadian Subscription & Delivery Benchmark

This document is a reference benchmark of the Canadian subscription and delivery service landscape, produced as a competitive intelligence artifact alongside the tools in this repo. It covers 11 delivery and grocery services and 14 subscription products across streaming, music, gaming, and cloud storage. All pricing is in Canadian dollars (CAD) and was verified in Q2 2026.

|---|---|---|---|---|

## Methodology

Pricing was sourced directly from each service's Canadian storefront or app. All figures are in CAD and were verified in Q2 2026. Annual pricing has been converted to a monthly equivalent where relevant for comparison. Services without an annual plan are monthly-only and marked N/A in the annual column.
